# Corrections and review boundary

This is an AI-assisted research proof, reorganized into a reading edition.
Its exact arithmetic, positive witnesses, and finite parity domains have
automated verification. It has not received independent external mathematical
review or proof-assistant certification. An independently implemented checker
does not mean an independent human reviewer. Novelty has not been assessed.

## Corrections carried into this edition

- A quintic path triple has a common tail label. If its omitted edges are
  01,12,23 on five labels, label 4 occurs in all three tails. Removing it
  gives the quartic path 23,03,01. A split has no common tail. The weighted
  count uses this corrected bijection.
- A quartic growth leaf must separate at least 89 repeated values to
  contradict T4<=88. The verifier rejects a weakened threshold of 87 or 88.
- The general split assignment explicitly replaces leaves that previously
  relied on ordinary quartic cap two. Cap three is the general premise.
- Seven-point and balanced parity enumerations require explicit completion
  of every order. Historical timeouts are not exclusions, and all 58
  six-point survivors must pass through the extension check.
- The two-triangle certificate closes its stated component. The elementary
  terminal-edge argument closes the other two small forests. An incomplete
  discovery tree is not presented as a proof of either component.

## Suggested mathematical review

Read the main proof, then check the midpoint argument and lift bijections
in Counting. Check the general assignment's replacement of cap-two leaves,
the no-new-ordinary hypotheses of credit leaves, and the forest coverage.
For the even branch, check the small-set reduction, normalization, subgroup
cases, and all preimages under nonunit multiplication. The dependency ledger
and source map make each retained claim traceable.
